YOUR 5-STEP WRITING FRAME
  1. Claim: State whether the cases are similar, different or mixed.
  2. Case A evidence: Mention one exact detail from the first case.
  3. Case B evidence: Mention one exact detail from the second case.
  4. Reasoning: Explain how the evidence supports your comparison.
  5. Limitation: Say what extra data or viewpoint could change your conclusion.
